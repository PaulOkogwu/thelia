# Thelia shop (home page)

Scope: the one-page shop at `/` plus `/thank-you`. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: young women and students (18-25, Western), gift buyers and market shoppers, arriving from Instagram/TikTok on phones. Job: fall for a bag, see the price, buy it with shipping in a few taps (Stripe Payment Link per bag). Proof/content: her real photos, her own first-person story and values, 11 named bags with prices. Constraints: no invented descriptions, testimonials or claims; Buy shows "Coming soon" until links arrive; sold state from data.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every bag is presented the way it leaves her hands: hung with her black swing tag, sewn with her white woven label, named and priced. Refuses the boutique-theme banner-plus-grid and the cream-and-serif "artisan" default.

OWN-WORLD: Optic-white label ground (#FAFAF8) and matte swing-tag black (#141414) as the two fields, charcoal ink (#262624), one deep rose thread accent (#7A1F3D) only on Buy. Components: woven name labels with folded, stitched ends (bag names and prices), black tag cards with a grommet and string, stitch-dashed rules. Colour comes from the bags alone.

STORY: Visitor meets the brand (tag), sees bags and prices within the first scroll, picks one and buys; then reads her story, values and the maker; ends in the catalogue and a tag-black footer.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White field; a large black swing tag hangs from a string off the top edge, centred, swaying slightly: inside it her logo in white, "Welcome to Thelia", her line, and a white-label "Shop the bags" button. Small logo icon top-left in the header. The shop's first bags (the clutch photos with their sewn labels) peek in at the bottom edge; her cut-out PNGs arrived too small to use here.

FORM: The Woven Label, position 1 on the ordered list (picked over the rolled zine). Seed key c444ce4b.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the hero tag swings on its string in response to scroll and pointer (static under reduced motion); bag cards carry their woven label with name and price, and Buy is a rose-stitched label button.

Unresolved: domain, Instagram/email, shipping rates and regions, returns policy, one-of-a-kind vs made to order, "Soleli" vs "Soleil".
